Cairn Table

Character sheet, Warden dashboard and serverless real-time multiplayer for the tabletop role-playing game Cairn (2nd Edition) — free, in your browser, no sign-up.

Build an adventurer with the guided creation wizard, track Hit Protection, attributes, gold and scars, manage a ten-slot inventory, and roll saves and damage. Open a room as Warden and see every player's sheet live — peer-to-peer over WebRTC, no account and no game server. Available in English and German.
